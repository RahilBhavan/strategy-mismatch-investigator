Revision 3, 2026-10-24. Part of the [POC specification](SPEC.md). These replace revision 2's mandatory live-integration criteria; they do not turn unperformed live checks into passes.

Live Strategy behavior remains untested; platform limits and independent-review qualifications are retained in that receipt.

- **AC-1 — Source labeling:** every comparison/evidence view and exported packet identifies “Proof of concept — simulated Strategy connection.” Live connection status is “not tested.” No fabricated deployment, authentication success or native installation is shown.
- **AC-2 — Boundary:** unsupported report types, different projects, malformed inputs and cases exceeding 100,000 records or 20 MB are rejected before diagnosis. Errors identify invalid fields and retain entered selections.
- **AC-3 — Adapter contract:** a versioned contract note identifies the report-context/execution operations, official documentation references, locally designed fields and unverified deployment behavior. Injected denial/session-expiry conditions produce the specified failure state with no privileged fallback or values from denied evidence. This does not pass actual Strategy identity checks.
- **AC-4 — Original computation:** on the original saved synthetic fixture, all-status A=220 and paid-only B=180 are computed from records and report selections, not stored as final-answer constants. A reviewer changes a source amount; both totals update according to the respective included records. Check browser and exported execution evidence.
- **AC-5 — Diagnostic experiment:** on the fixed original fixture, changing only the transient status selection reproduces impact 40 and residual 0. Evidence includes original and variant selections, instance identifiers and computed values. A changed fixture produces the independently expected new impact.
- **AC-6 — Metric context:** captured metric identity/definition, report selections, units, grain and snapshot are visible in evidence. A different definition or missing result-affecting context produces candidate/unresolved status rather than business-equivalence approval.
- **AC-7 — Original preservation:** original synthetic report definitions and input records have matching before/after content fingerprints. Diagnostic selections belong to a separate transient instance; no original definition or warehouse query is edited.
- **AC-8 — Comparability:** differing units, grain or snapshot identities prevent a definitive explanation. Equal timestamps alone do not establish shared data context. Check fixed-context, conflicting-context and missing-context fixtures.
- **AC-9 — Negative controls:** identical captured inputs/results produce “No numerical difference in these captured results.” Different definitions with equal totals do not receive a semantic-equivalence label. Check both controls.
- **AC-10 — Ambiguity:** an interacting multi-difference fixture yields separate candidates and unresolved residual unless isolated computations establish their effects. Candidate impacts are not added as independent when they overlap.
- **AC-11 — Evidence status:** structural differences alone are “candidate”; a controlled result in comparable synthetic context permits “numerical cause demonstrated on synthetic data”; unavailable/unexecuted checks are “not tested.” All three states appear in the test suite and export.
- **AC-12 — Numeric integrity:** totals use declared aggregation semantics and exact unit/scale representation; null remains distinct from zero; percentage delta is unavailable for A=0. Overflow or incomplete/paginated result markers produce an error rather than a partial total. Check arithmetic and completeness controls.
- **AC-13 — Failure lifecycle:** injected timeout, rate limit, denial, expired session and network failure produce their specified states without a partial cause claim. Retry is bounded to at most one automatic retry for a declared transient failure, respects a supplied Retry-After within the configured deadline, and never automatically retries a denial. Synthetic execution timeout is 5 seconds.
- **AC-14 — Recovery:** retrying an unchanged case creates no duplicate findings. Changed source context invalidates previous conclusions. A locally exported synthetic packet reopens after application restart with original context, provenance and status. Check retry/edit/restart behavior.
- **AC-15 — Interface:** at 390px and 1120px the compare/evidence/unknown flows have no horizontal page overflow; controls are keyboard reachable with visible focus. The diagnostic experiment and its outcome can be inspected without opening developer tools.
- **AC-16 — Local evidence:** the demonstration uses synthetic data only, completes with external network access disabled and requires no account or paid service. Export contains no auth material or real customer data. Exports and browser state retain the simulated-source label.
- **AC-17 — Held-out check:** independently author at least 6 unseen supported single-cause cases, 2 equal-total controls and 2 ambiguous/incomplete cases. Each asserted numerical cause matches its reference; other cases remain unresolved/equal as expected. Record correctness, abstention and failure counts. No mandatory live endpoint is included in this POC criterion.
- **AC-18 — Product overlap:** the review note explains existing Strategy comparison capabilities and what this POC adds: a measured cause-specific experiment plus its evidence. It labels customer value and competitive uniqueness unverified and records which existing capabilities a future integration should reuse.
- **AC-19 — Instance lifecycle:** completed, canceled and failed simulated attempts account for their transient instances without deleting another attempt's instances. Cancellation cannot produce a finished diagnosis later. The live equivalent is separately marked “not tested” until actual server cleanup/expiry and cancellation behavior are exercised.
